The original and only official DATUM Gateway repository is OCEAN-xyz/datum_gateway at https://github.com/OCEAN-xyz/datum_gateway

Official DATUM supports Bitcoin only, using Bitcoin’s consensus rules and SHA-256d proof of work.

DATUM (including the DATUM Gateway, DATUM Prime, and the DATUM Protocol) was originally written by Jason Hughes for the OCEAN Bitcoin Mining Pool.

DATUM Gateway was released under the MIT open-source license in October 2024.
